# Fitness & Hypertrophy Principles

## 1. Progressive Overload
To build muscle (hypertrophy), you must gradually increase the stress placed on the body during exercise. This can be done by:
- Increasing the weight lifted.
- Increasing the number of repetitions.
- Decreasing rest time between sets.
- Improving exercise technique.

## 2. Training Volume
Volume is defined as Sets x Reps x Weight. For most people, performing 10-20 hard sets per muscle group per week is optimal for growth.

## 3. Rest and Recovery
Muscle grows during rest, not during the workout. Aim for:
- 48-72 hours of rest between training the same muscle group.
- 7-9 hours of quality sleep per night.

## 4. Nutrition for Muscle Growth
- **Protein**: Aim for 1.6g to 2.2g of protein per kilogram of body weight.
- **Caloric Surplus**: To gain muscle effectively, consume slightly more calories than you burn (200-300 kcal surplus).
- **Hydration**: Drink at least 3 liters of water daily.

## 5. Exercise Selection
- **Compound Movements**: Exercises that involve multiple joints (Squats, Bench Press, Deadlifts, Rows). These should form the core of your routine.
- **Isolation Movements**: Exercises that target a single muscle (Bicep Curls, Lateral Raises). Use these to supplement compound lifts.

## 6. Routine Structures
- **Full Body**: Good for beginners or those only able to train 2-3 days a week.
- **Upper/Lower Split**: Common for 4 days a week.
- **Push/Pull/Legs (PPL)**: Popular for 3 or 6 days a week.

## 7. Importance of Consistency
Optimal results come from staying consistent over months and years, rather than intense but sporadic training sessions.
